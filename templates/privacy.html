Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Spendly handles your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name and email address when you register for an account, and the expense data you log to track your finances.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the expense tracking service, maintain your account, and improve our platform's features. We do not use your financial data for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely for your own personal financial tracking.

5. Your Rights

You may access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information at any time through your account settings.
